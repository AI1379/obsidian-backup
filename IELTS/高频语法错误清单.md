# 高频语法错误清单

> [!info] 来源
> GPT 对历次 Task 1 / Task 2 批改与口语模考的横向语法诊断（2026-09-01）。原文 16 条，此处按问题类型重组。
> 相关：[[中式表达对照表]]（表达层错误）· [[Task 1 写法]]（时态与 figure for）· [[Part1模考反馈笔记]] / [[Part2-3模考反馈笔记]]（口语同款错误）

---

## 一、核心诊断：缺的不是 range，是 control

- **"你现在缺的不是 grammar range，而是 grammar control。"**
- Range 其实不贫乏：although、because、which、定语从句、被动、比较结构、过去完成时、条件句都已经会用。
- 真正的瓶颈是这些"小东西"反复出错：a/an/the、单复数、is/are、provide/provides、although/but、despite/although、of/for、doing/to do。
- 评分同时看 **range + accuracy**：**15 个复杂句里 8 个有小错，不如 10 个清晰句。**
- 好消息：把错误率从"每两三句一个"压到"每五六句一个"，比短期硬学一堆新句型现实得多。

### 口语和写作的病根不同，药方相反

| | 口语 | 写作 |
|---|---|---|
| 病根 | **说到一半修改句型**（despite…→although…、maybe…because…、I think…I think…），本来会的语法被自己改坏 | **为追求复杂度主动把句子写复杂**，错误概率随之上升 |
| 药方 | **先说出来，不要回头修改** | **写完以后主动删掉不必要的复杂结构** |

## 二、句子结构类

### 1. 一句话塞太多连接关系（写作+口语共同头号问题）

典型模式已经固化：**观点 → because → 原因 → which → 后果**

- ❌ I think music can calm me because it makes me relaxed which can help me...
- ✅ I think music can calm me because it helps me relax.（前半句已完整，还想加就另起一句）
- ✅ It also helps me forget about stress for a while.

> 简单句 + because ／ 简单句 + which ／ 简单句，通常比"主句+because+从句+which+从句+and+从句"安全得多。6 分语法**不要求**一句话套三四层从句。

**强迫自己轮换**（别每句都是 because→which）：

- I think **X because Y**.
- One reason is that **Y**.
- This means that **Z**.
- For example, ...
- As a result, ...

### 2. although / but / despite 混用

- ❌ Although it is small, **but** I really like it. → ✅ **Although it is small, I really like it.**（although 本身已含"虽然"，不再加 but；中文"虽然…但是…"不逐字翻译）
- ❌ **despite it is small** → ✅ despite its small size / despite being small / although it is small

> [!note] 只记这一条
> **although + 完整句子；despite + 名词 / -ing。**
> 写作里这类错误是明显的 Accuracy 扣分点，务必避免。

### 3. which 滥用（标志性问题）

两个坑：

- **先行词不清**：I like photos because they can remind me of my family, **which**…（which 指 photos？remind me？还是整件事？模糊。）
- **which 套娃**：❌ Many people move to cities, which makes them far away from their families, which causes...

拆句写：

> Many people move to other cities for work. As a result, they have to live far away from their families.

> [!note]
> **As a result 并不比 which 低级**，IELTS 不会因为少写两个 which 就扣分。

## 三、小语法高频错误（🔴 第一优先级）

### 4. 单复数与可数名词

| ❌ | ✅ | 规则 |
|---|---|---|
| solve **problem** | solve **problems** / solve **a problem** | 单数可数名词不能裸奔 |
| write program | write programs / write a program | 同上 |
| **thousands people** | thousands **of** people | 概数加 of；**three thousand people**（确数不加 of） |
| nature **sceneries** | natural scenery | scenery 不可数 |
| West Lake is **a beautiful scenery** | West Lake has beautiful scenery / is a beautiful place | 同上（Part 2-3 模考同款） |

口诀：**thousands of / millions of / hundreds of 概数加 of；具体数字直接接名词。**

### 5. 冠词 a / an / the

脑子盯着内容词时最容易漏冠词：

- ❌ I had discussion about whether AI... → ✅ I had **a** discussion...
- ❌ I am university student → ✅ I am **a** university student.

> [!tip] 自检法（写完必做）
> 扫一遍所有**单数可数名词**（government / student / city / problem / reason / device…），逐个问：**前面有没有 a / an / the / my / this / one 等限定词？**
> 看到 `student needs...` 要立刻警觉 → a student / the student / students。

### 6. 主谓一致：第三人称单数掉 -s

- ❌ internet provide... → ✅ the Internet **provides**...
- ❌ technology make... → ✅ technology **makes**...

好消息：这不是不懂规则，是**来不及监控**——写作里靠检查即可解决。

**重点盯这些主语后面**：it / technology / the number / the government / this method → 接 **is / has / makes / provides / allows**，而不是 are / have / make / provide / allow。

### 7. 滥用 there is / are（受中文"有"影响）

❌ there is lots of bikes **stopped** in the parking lot
✅ There **are** lots of bikes **parked** in the parking lot.

一句话暴露三个点：

1. bikes 复数 → there **are**
2. 停放的自行车用 **parked**（不是 stopped）
3. lots of + 复数

> [!important] 这句是全场缩影
> **你不是不知道任何一个语法点，而是实时组合时同时漏了几个。** 所以解法不是学新知识，是放慢 + 检查。

## 四、词汇形式类（🟠 第二优先级）

### 8. 词性弄错

- ❌ math **researches** → ✅ mathematical **research**（research 通常不可数）
- mathematician / mathematics / mathematical 临场易混，固定搭配：
  - **a mathematics problem**
  - **mathematical research**
  - **a mathematician**
- ❌ 把名词当动词：innovation → ✅ produce innovations / create new ideas / make discoveries

### 9. 动词后面的形式（doing / to do）

❌ **enjoy to do** → ✅ enjoy **doing**

IELTS 高频结构清单（稳定下来错误会少很多）：

| 接 to do | 接 doing | 接原形/其他 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| want to do | enjoy doing | help somebody (do)（help me relax 更自然，help me to relax 也可） |
| need to do | avoid doing | make somebody do |
| prefer to do / prefer doing | spend time doing | allow somebody **to** do |
| | | encourage somebody **to** do |

### 10. 介词：figure for / number of

固定搭配，别再犹豫：

- **the figure for** X → the figure for the coast / for China / for women
- **the number of** visitors ｜ **the proportion of** people ｜ **the percentage of** students

> number **of** + 人/物；figure **for** + 类别。（❌ the figure ~~of~~ the coast）

### 11. 代词指代漂移（口语尤甚）

- ❌ if I see **these photos**, I will... **this photo** will remind me of...（复数→单数中途切换）
- ❌ technology **is**... → 后面变成 **they are**...
  - 指 technology 本身 → **it**
  - 指 technological devices → **they**

## 五、Task 1 专项

### 12. 时态：整体知道，稳定性不够

- ❌ The mountains **had been** the second choice in 1987 but was overtaken... → ✅ **were** the second most popular destination in 1987 but **were overtaken** by the lakes in 1997.
- 全故事都在过去，**simple past 就够了**；had done 只留给"过去的过去"（By 2002, the number had risen to 75,000.）。
- 详细判断规则 → [[Task 1 写法]] §六「完成时：两步判断，三个结论」。

### 13. 比较结构：意思对但英语不自然

| ❌ | ✅ |
|---|---|
| became the second one at last | became the second most popular destination / **ranked second** / finished the period in second place |
| reached a peak of 75 thousand, higher than the figure of the coast that year | It reached a peak of 75,000 in 2002, which was higher than the figure **for** the coast. |

### 14. 翻译腔（不算语法错，但"像翻译出来的英语"）

| ❌ | ✅ |
|---|---|
| have a trend of increasing but may decrease during some particular periods | increased in all three areas, although there were some fluctuations |
| became the second one at last | finished the period in second place |

> 从 5.5 往 6 / 6.5 提时，sentence construction 会越来越重要。

## 六、优先级总表

| 优先级 | 问题 | 典型错误 |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴 1 | 单复数 | solve problem / thousands people |
| 🔴 2 | 冠词 | I am university student |
| 🔴 3 | 主谓一致 | technology provide |
| 🔴 4 | although / despite | although..., but / despite it is |
| 🟠 5 | which 滥用 | because..., which... |
| 🟠 6 | 动词形式 | doing / to do 混乱 |
| 🟠 7 | 介词 | figure of → figure for |

**现在完全可以不学**：倒装、虚拟语气、分词独立结构。

## 七、写作总原则

> [!important] 考场默念
> **One sentence, one main job.** 一句话只完成一个任务。

Task 2 示范：

❌

> I think people should live with their families because family members can provide emotional support which is important for people who work under pressure and this can also make society more stable.

✅

> I think living close to family members has several benefits. The most important one is emotional support. People who work under pressure can talk to their families when they feel stressed. This can significantly improve their well-being.

看起来"高级语法"变少了，但**这才是更接近 6.5 的英语**——因为你在控制语言，而不是被句子拖着跑。
