# 作文批改 · Task 1 · 澳大利亚出口

> 题目：线图：澳大利亚出口去往 Japan / US / China / India 的占比 1990–2012 · 2026-08-30 批改（用时约 20 分钟）
> 上一篇：[[作文批改 Task 1 - 英国燃料产量]]
> 结构 / 分组 / 换词原则见 [[Task 1 写法]]

## 总评

- **6.0–6.5，偏 6.0**；比上一篇燃料图更清楚——已主动抓到核心结构：**两降（Japan/US）、两升（China/India）、China 取代 Japan 成最大出口市场**
- 20 分钟完成，速度完全合格
- 主要问题不是看图，而是表达准确性和细节选择
- 分项预估：TA 6.5 / CC 6.0–6.5 / LR 6.0 / GRA 5.5–6.0 → **约 6.0–6.5**

## Overview 修一下就很好

| 原句 | 问题 | 改法 |
|------|------|------|
| `the percentage ... to Japan was the most` | percentage 不能说 was the most | `Japan had the highest percentage` / `Japan accounted for the largest share` |
| `but was replaced by China in 2012` | "被替换"机械 | `China had overtaken Japan by 2012.` |

> Overall, the shares of Australian exports going to Japan and the US declined over the period, whereas those going to China and India increased. Japan was Australia's largest export market in 1990, but China had overtaken it by the end of the period.

## 分组：下降组 + 上升组

一路 Japan → US → China → India → Japan/China 没错，但这道题有现成的两个主体段分组：

- **Body 1 下降组**：Japan + US
- **Body 2 上升组**：China + India

## 分段批改

### Introduction：量说错了（又是老问题）

| 原句 | 问题 | 改法 |
|------|------|------|
| `the amount of Australian export with four countries` | 图是 percentage 不是 amount；exports **to** countries 不是 with | `The line graph illustrates the percentage of Australian exports going to Japan, the US, China and India between 1990 and 2012.`（`share of exports` 也适合这张图） |

### Body 1（下降组）

| 原句 | 问题 | 改法 |
|------|------|------|
| `Japan was the first export country of Australia` | 中式"第一出口国" | `Japan was Australia's largest export market.`（**export market** 直接记） |
| `More than 25% of Australian productions were exported to Japan` | 数据含义错误：分母是 exports 不是 production | `Japan accounted for more than 25% of Australian exports.`（account for 已会用） |
| `the percentage ... to Japan decreased steadily` | 缺终点，只写"下降"显得没落地 | `Japan's share of Australian exports declined steadily, reaching around 18% by 2012.` |
| `the percentage ... fell as well, but with a peak in 2000` | US 不是一路下降：12→9→12→7 | `The figure for the US fluctuated somewhat, reaching a peak of around 12% in 2000 before declining to approximately 7% in 2012.`（**fluctuated before declining** 更准确） |

### Body 2（上升组）

| 原句 | 问题 | 改法 |
|------|------|------|
| `the percentage ... to China and India are both lower than 5%` | 过去时 | `In 1990, the figures for China and India were both below 5%.` |
| `the amount of Australian exports to China started to increase rapidly in 2000` | 又滑回 amount；in/after | `China's share began to rise rapidly after 2000.`（图测的是 share；after 2000 比 in 2000 准确） |
| `The percentage to India also started to increase notably but not so rapidly as to China` | 结构绕 | `India's share also rose, although much more gradually than China's.`（值得掌握的比较句） |
| `In 2010 China became the main export country` | 交叉实际约在 2007–2008，别猜具体年份 | `By 2010, China had clearly overtaken Japan to become Australia's largest export market.`（`by 2010` 安全） |

### 结尾缺口：停在 2010，图到 2012

把终值带出来收尾：

> By 2012, China accounted for around 30% of Australian exports, compared with approximately 18% for Japan.

India 的尾部变化别漏（2010 ≈8% → 2012 ≈6%），不能让读者以为一直涨到 2012：

> India's share also increased considerably, reaching about 8% in 2010 before falling slightly to around 6% in 2012.

## 参考修订版（约 180 词）

> The line graph illustrates the percentage of Australian exports going to Japan, the US, China and India between 1990 and 2012.
>
> Overall, the shares of Australian exports to Japan and the US declined over the period, whereas those to China and India increased. Japan was Australia's largest export market in 1990, but China had overtaken it by the end of the period.
>
> In 1990, Japan accounted for approximately 27% of Australian exports, considerably higher than the figure for the US, at around 12%. Japan's share then declined steadily, falling to about 18% in 2012. The figure for the US fluctuated somewhat, reaching around 12% again in 2000 before decreasing to approximately 7% at the end of the period.
>
> By contrast, China and India both accounted for less than 5% of Australian exports in 1990. China's share began to rise rapidly after 2000, reaching roughly 30% by 2012 and making China Australia's largest export market. India's figure also increased, although much more gradually, peaking at around 8% in 2010 before falling slightly to approximately 6% in 2012.

## 该记的 5 个表达（已收进 [[IELTS/Vocabulary/写作主动词汇|写作主动词汇]]）

`account for X% of exports` · `Australia's largest export market` · `the figure/share for...` · `fluctuate before declining` · `overtake A to become...`

## 检查清单（最后几分钟专查两件事）

- 我写的是 **percentage**，还是不小心变成了 amount / production？
- 历史图是不是所有数据动词都保持**过去时**？

本篇的 `amount`、`productions`、`are both lower` 最后扫一遍都有机会抓出来。
