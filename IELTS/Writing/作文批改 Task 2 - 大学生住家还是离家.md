## 分段批改

### Introduction

| 原句 | 问题 | 改法 |
|------|------|------|
| `while in the other countries students attend...` | 几乎照抄题干，要 paraphrase；泛指不加 the | `University students in some countries continue living with their families, whereas those in others move to another city to study.` |
| `It is debateable that if the advantages...` | 拼写 debatable；`It is debatable whether...`（不用 that if） | 更直接：`Although living away from home has some drawbacks, I believe its benefits outweigh the disadvantages.` |

### 第一主体段：论点成立，但要"从题目角度写"

题目问的是 living **away** from home 的 disadvantages，所以段首最好直接扣题：

> Living away from home can present several difficulties for university students.

再展开：适应新环境、缺家人照顾、自己做饭清洁、宿舍少隐私。

| 原句                                                                           | 问题                | 改法                                                                                         |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `There is no needs for university students living at home to get used to...` | `no need` 是固定说法   | `Students who stay with their families do not need to adapt to an unfamiliar environment.` |
| `focus on academy`                                                           | academy = 学院，不是学业 | `focus on their studies` / `focus on academic work`                                        |
| `daily staffs like house cleaning or meals`                                  | staff = 工作人员      | `cooking, cleaning and other household chores`（**chores = 日常杂务**，直接记）                      |
| `living in dormitory`                                                        | 要冠词               | `living in a dormitory` / `living in university accommodation`                             |

### 第二主体段：全文最好的部分

两个论点（Independence + Social connections）都适合本题，且互不重复。

| 原句 | 问题 | 改法 |
|------|------|------|
| `A person cannot rely on their family for a whole life` | 搭配 | `People cannot rely on their families throughout their lives.` 更贴题：`Young adults eventually need to learn to live independently.` |
| `offers a chance to put the students into a new environment and push them to be independent` | put into 硬 | `places students in a new environment and encourages them to become more independent`（记 **encourage sb to do sth**） |
| `students who live at home for nearly twenty years` | 时态（上大学前已住了近二十年） | `young people who have previously depended heavily on their families`（还能回避时态） |
| `is also a chance for them to communicate with others` | 表达 | `gives students more opportunities to interact with their peers`（简单版：socialise with other students） |
| `It will be much easier for them to establish social connections with each other if they not only study together but also live together` | 结构绕 | `Students who live and study together may find it easier to form close friendships.` |
| `the social network will become a precious resource` | 商业/游戏资源感 | `These relationships may remain valuable even after graduation.` |

### Conclusion

| 原句 | 问题 | 改法 |
|------|------|------|
| `the benefits ... matters more` | 主语复数 → matter | 用题目给的词：`the benefits of living away from home outweigh the disadvantages` |

> In conclusion, although living at home offers greater comfort and family support, I believe the benefits of living away from home outweigh the disadvantages because it promotes independence and social development.

## 保留原思路的改稿

> University students in some countries continue living with their families, whereas those in others move to another city to study. Although living away from home has some disadvantages, I believe that its benefits outweigh the drawbacks.
>
> Living away from home can present several difficulties for university students. Those who remain with their families can study in a familiar environment and do not need to adapt to a completely new lifestyle. Family support may also allow them to focus more on their studies without worrying as much about cooking, cleaning and other household chores. In addition, students living at home are more likely to have their own private space, which may be difficult to obtain in a shared dormitory.
>
> However, I believe that living away from home provides more significant long-term benefits. Young adults eventually need to learn to live independently rather than rely on their families throughout their lives. Studying in another city places students in a new environment and encourages them to take responsibility for their own daily lives. Furthermore, living in a dormitory gives students more opportunities to socialise with their peers. Students who both live and study together may find it easier to form close friendships, and these relationships may remain valuable even after graduation.
>
> In conclusion, although living at home provides comfort and family support, I believe that the benefits of living away from home outweigh the disadvantages, particularly because it helps university students become more independent and develop stronger social connections.

注意：这个版本**几乎没有增加高级词汇**，主要变化是：

- `focus on academy` → `focus on their studies`
- `daily staffs` → `household chores`
- `for a whole life` → `throughout their lives`
- `push them to be independent` → `encourage them to become independent`
- `precious resource` → `remain valuable`

## 核心结论：新的阶段目标

这篇验证了之前的判断——本篇的概念（family support / daily tasks / privacy / independence / social connections）都很简单，但**完全足够支撑 Task 2**。继续保持：

> **论点可以成熟，但英文表达尽量简单。**

不再追求"我还能写得多复杂"，而是**让已经写得出来的东西越来越正确**。剩下的 10 分钟与其再加一个论点，不如专查 `There is no needs` / `focus on academy` / `if they not only...` / `benefits ... matters` 这类错误。
