# 作文批改 · Task 2 · 工作满意度与稳定

> 题目：job satisfaction 和 job security 哪个更重要，讨论双方观点并给出自己的看法 · 2026-08-30 批改

## 总评

- 约 **256 词**，字数刚好够，不要再缩

## 分段批改

### Introduction：表达不自然

| 原句 | 问题 | 改法 |
|------|------|------|
| `a hot spot in jobs market` | hot spot 像"热点地区 / Wi-Fi 热点" | `Whether job satisfaction or job security is more important has long been debated.` |
| `the job security` | 泛指抽象概念不加 the | `job security` / `job satisfaction` |
| `job satisfaction cannot always be satisfied` | "满意度不能被满意" | `cannot always be achieved` / `people cannot always expect to find a satisfying job` |
| `From my perspect of view` | perspect 拼错；`from my perspective` 和 `from my point of view` 二选一，不能混 | `In my view, job satisfaction is more important.` |

### 第二段：job security 这边思路合格，表达要修

论点本身没问题（先要生存 → 必要时牺牲满意度换稳定收入）：

| 原句 | 问题 | 改法 |
|------|------|------|
| `permanent job is somehow easier for most of us` | 逻辑偏了，题目说的是"不能总指望有满意度" | `Supporters of job security argue that people cannot always expect to find work that they enjoy.` |
| `To earn sufficient resources to survive` | 太像游戏里的 resource system | `earn enough money to support themselves` / `meet their basic needs` |
| `sacrifice the job satisfaction` | the + 表达生硬 | `sacrifice some degree of job satisfaction` |
| `A job with high security` | 有现成形容词就用 | `a secure job`（不要都抽象成 high security / high satisfaction） |
| `benificial` | 拼写，反复错 | `beneficial`（benefit → beneficial，记死） |
| `beneficial to life stability` | 中式 | `provide financial stability` |
| `economy delimma` | economy→economic，delimma→dilemma，且根本不用 dilemma | `without constantly worrying about losing their income` |

整段可以这样写：

> Supporters of job security argue that people cannot always expect to find work that they enjoy. In order to support themselves and their families, they may sometimes need to accept a less satisfying but more secure job. Permanent employment can provide a stable income and greater financial security, reducing the stress associated with unemployment or an uncertain career.

### 第三段：核心思想好，语言重灾区

Maslow 层次需求的思路（生存/安全 → 更高层的心理满足）可以，但**没必要写成心理学理论**。雅思奖励"把论证解释清楚"，不是"我知道某个理论"：

| 原句 | 问题 | 改法 |
|------|------|------|
| `Some modern psychology shows that the mental demand is a higher level of demand than physical demand.` | 不自然，还无必要地声称学术事实 | `Once people's basic financial needs are met, factors such as personal fulfilment, a sense of achievement and enjoyment of one's work can become increasingly important.` |
| `achievement feeling deep from heart` | 硬造 | `a sense of achievement`（直接收下的 chunk） |
| `An unsatisfied but secure job` | -ed / -ing 分不清 | `an unsatisfying but secure job`（unsatisfied = 人感到不满意；unsatisfying = 事令人不满意） |
| `keep a life quality that is not bad` | 中式 | `maintain a reasonable standard of living`（standard of living 超常见搭配） |
| `the tireness and mental pressure created by jobs with satisfaction may not be so much as less satisfied jobs` | tireness→tiredness；整句结构失控 | `People who are satisfied with their work may experience less stress and emotional exhaustion than those who dislike their jobs.` |
| `definitely beneficial` | 绝对副词没必要 | `which can be beneficial to their mental health.` |

### 最后一个论点：删掉或改写

> 有时两个都能满足，不必二选一

逻辑没错，但题目问"**哪个更重要**"，最后说"不用选"会削弱自己的论证。改成和立场一致的版本：

> Although an ideal job may provide both security and satisfaction, when a choice has to be made, I would give greater importance to satisfaction once basic financial needs have been met.

### Conclusion

| 原句 | 问题 | 改法 |
|------|------|------|
| `Overall` | Task 2 用 `In conclusion`（Overall 更像 Task 1 的 overview） | `In conclusion` |
| `the job security is important` | 泛指不加 the | `job security` |
| `may be more important` | 正文已明确表态，结尾立场突然变弱 | `is more important` |

> In conclusion, although job security is important for financial stability, I believe job satisfaction is more important in the long term because it contributes to personal fulfilment and mental well-being.

## 保留原思路的改稿（≈ Band 7）

> People have different views about whether job satisfaction or job security is more important. Some argue that people cannot always expect to find satisfying work and should therefore prioritise permanent employment. In my view, however, job satisfaction is more important.
>
> Supporters of job security argue that people cannot always find work that they genuinely enjoy. In order to support themselves and their families, they may sometimes need to accept a less satisfying but more secure job. Permanent employment can provide a stable income and greater financial security, reducing the stress associated with unemployment and allowing people to maintain a reasonable standard of living.
>
> However, I believe that job satisfaction is ultimately more important. Once people's basic financial needs are met, personal fulfilment and a sense of achievement can become increasingly important. An unsatisfying but secure job may provide financial stability, but it can leave employees feeling unmotivated and dissatisfied with their lives. By contrast, people who enjoy their work may experience less stress and be more motivated to perform well, which can also benefit their mental well-being. Although an ideal job would provide both satisfaction and security, I would prioritise satisfaction when basic financial needs have already been met.
>
> In conclusion, while job security is important because it provides financial stability, I believe that job satisfaction is more important in the long term because it contributes to personal fulfilment and well-being.

## 核心结论

最大的问题已经变成：**脑中想到复杂中文概念后，逐词翻译成英语**，而英语往往有现成 chunk：

| 想表达的中文 | 硬造 | 现成 chunk |
|------|------|-----------|
| 内心深处的成就感 | achievement feeling deep from heart | a sense of achievement |
| 生活稳定 | life stability | financial stability |
| 生活质量还不错 | a life quality that is not bad | a reasonable standard of living |

这篇只记 7 个（已同步进 [[IELTS/Vocabulary/写作主动词汇|写作主动词汇]]）：

`job satisfaction / job security` · `financial stability` · `meet one's basic needs` · `a reasonable standard of living` · `personal fulfilment` · `a sense of achievement` · `mental well-being`

下次碰到 work / money / happiness / career 题，这一套几乎全能复用。错误对照已收进 [[中式表达对照表]]。
