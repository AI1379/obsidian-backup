# 作文批改 · Task 2 · 死刑

> 题目：死刑是否应保留（capital punishment）· 2026-08-11 GPT 批改
> 上一篇：[[作文批改 Task 2 - 运动]]

## 总评

- 约 **260 词**，刚过 250，长度够
- 用时 40 分钟出头，基本符合考试节奏
- 预估 **6.0 左右，发挥好可摸 6.5**
- 失分点：**Lexical Resource 和 Grammar Accuracy**，而非观点
- 进步点：论证已有"自己的东西"，论点丰富

## 立场（可以保留）

> 死刑有风险也有局限，但对最严重犯罪仍应保留，且必须严格限制。

比简单的 good/bad 更容易形成有层次的文章。

## 最需要改的第一件事：反方段落只列观点，没展开

已有两个反方论点，但第二点（误判不可逆）没展开：

> human can never revive ❌

展开模板 **Point → Explanation → Consequence**：

> Opponents of capital punishment argue that it violates the basic right to life. More importantly, judicial systems are not perfect, and an innocent person may occasionally be wrongly convicted. Unlike imprisonment, a death sentence cannot be reversed once it has been carried out, even if new evidence later proves the person innocent.

直接记：

- **an execution is irreversible**
- **a wrongly executed person cannot be brought back to life**
- **a wrongful conviction may have irreversible consequences**

`irreversible` 非常适合这道题。

## 分句批改

### Introduction

- `Nowadays` 不必要 → 直接 `Some people believe...`
- `From my point of view, I believe...` 重复 → 留一个
- `to keep the safety of the society` → 中式搭配，英文一般说 `to protect/maintain public safety`
- 改为（还能直接预告核心立场）：
  > I believe that capital punishment should remain available for the most serious crimes, although its use must be strictly limited.

### 第二段

| 原句 | 问题 | 改法 |
|------|------|------|
| `is against fundamental human right` | 搭配 | `violates fundamental human rights`（human rights 常复数） |
| `one of the main concern` | 单复数 | `one of the main concerns` |
| `one of the main concern of abolishing...` | 逻辑绕 | `This is one of the main arguments for abolishing capital punishment.` |
| `human can never revive` | human 不能泛指人；revive 是"苏醒" | `an innocent person cannot be brought back to life if they are mistakenly executed`，雅思推荐抽象版 `Wrongful executions are irreversible.` |
| `Some people believes` | 主谓 | `Some people believe` |
| `regret for their crime` | 搭配 | `regret their crimes` / `feel remorse for their crimes`（`remorse`=悔恨，值得记） |
| `It is undoubtedly true, showing that...` | It 指代模糊 | `These concerns are valid and demonstrate that capital punishment has serious limitations.` |

补充逻辑（若写悔改/改造）：
> Some offenders may genuinely feel remorse and become rehabilitated. 立即剥夺生命就不给 rehabilitation 机会。

### 第三段

| 原句 | 问题 | 改法 |
|------|------|------|
| `it should be noticed that` | 不佳 | `However, it should also be recognised that...` 或 `However, supporters of capital punishment argue that...`（后者更符合 Discuss both views） |
| `it is the criminal that violates...victims can never revive` | 易显得情绪化 | `Those who support capital punishment may argue that offenders who deliberately take another person's life have themselves committed an irreversible violation of the victim's most basic rights.` |
| `comforts the family of victims` | 简单 | `provide a sense of justice or closure for victims' families` |
| `sends a warning to potential criminals` | 可更学术 | `act as a deterrent to potential offenders`（`deterrent`=威慑因素，值得记） |
| `People may not believe justice` | 表达 | `people may lose faith in the justice system`（值得背：`lose faith in`） |
| `kill others by intention` | 搭配 | `kill someone intentionally` / `deliberately take another person's life` |
| `leading to the probability of private punishment` | 无此说法 | `This could encourage some people to take the law into their own hands.`（`private punishment` → **vigilante justice** / **take the law into one's own hands**，后者对雅思更稳） |
| `become the soil of black society or other crime organization` | 中式直译 | `become a breeding ground for organised crime`；`black society` → `organised crime` / `criminal organisations` |

### 最后的 nuance 观点（很好，但语言要改）

> 即便支持死刑，也必须严格限制国家使用这种权力，避免误杀无辜。

- `limit the capital punishment` → `strictly limit the use of capital punishment`
- `sentence with caution` → `death sentences should be imposed with extreme caution`
- `prevent the government becomes` → 结构应为 `prevent A from doing B` → `prevent the state from executing innocent people`（法律语境 `state` 常优于 `government`）

### Conclusion

- `necessarity` 不是正常用词 → `capital punishment is necessary in certain cases` 或更高级 `there remains a case for retaining capital punishment`
- `should be limited to keep it is used on...` → `should be strictly limited to ensure that it is used only for the most serious crimes`（`ensure that` 是需要的表达）

## 保留原思路的改稿

> Some people believe that capital punishment should be abolished, while others argue that it should remain available for the most serious crimes. I believe that the death penalty is necessary in certain extreme cases, although its use must be strictly limited.
>
> Opponents of capital punishment argue that it is cruel and violates fundamental human rights. More importantly, judicial systems are not perfect, and innocent people may sometimes be wrongly convicted. Unlike imprisonment, an execution is irreversible once it has been carried out. Some offenders may also genuinely feel remorse and become rehabilitated. These concerns are valid and demonstrate that capital punishment has serious limitations.
>
> However, supporters of the death penalty may argue that people who deliberately commit the most serious crimes have themselves violated the basic rights of their victims. An appropriate punishment may provide a sense of justice for victims' families and may also act as a deterrent to potential offenders. Public confidence in the justice system could be undermined if people who intentionally take another person's life are perceived to receive insufficient punishment. In extreme cases, this may even encourage people to take the law into their own hands, harming social stability. Nevertheless, death sentences should be imposed with extreme caution in order to minimise the risk of executing innocent people.
>
> In conclusion, although capital punishment has serious limitations, I believe that it should remain available for the most serious crimes. However, its use should be strictly restricted and subject to rigorous legal safeguards.

## 核心结论

论点不缺（wrongful conviction / victims' rights / deterrence / lose faith in justice / vigilante justice / social stability / limiting state power 都能想到）。真正卡住的是 **概念 → 英文表达** 这一层，详见 [[中式表达对照表]] 与 [[写作主动词汇]]。
