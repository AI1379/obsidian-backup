# 作文批改 · Task 2 · 运动（身心强度）

> 题目：physical vs mental strength in sporting success · 2026-08-11 GPT 批改（log 第 1 段）
> 下一篇：[[作文批改 Task 2 - 死刑]]

## 总评

- 论证思路好：physical 是基础门槛，mental 在高水平竞技中是决定性因素 —— 合格的 IELTS argument
- 预估 **Task 2 约 6.0**
- 拖分的全是英语表达，观点不用改
- 规律总结：不是不认识单词，而是 **collocation / lexical choice 问题**（见文末）

## 分句批改

### Introduction

| 原句 | 问题 | 改法 |
|------|------|------|
| `plays more important role` | 缺冠词 | `plays a more important role` |
| `the physical strength / the mental strength` | 抽象概念通常不加 the | `physical strength` / `mental strength` |
| `mental strength is more emphasised` | `emphasise`=强调某事，词义错 | `I believe mental strength is more important` |

改完：
> People have different views about whether physical or mental strength plays a more important role in sporting success. While physical strength is clearly essential in many sports, I believe mental strength is more important, particularly at higher levels of competition.

### Body 1

| 原句 | 问题 | 改法 |
|------|------|------|
| `The physical ability...with no doubt` | the 多余；中式 | `Physical ability undoubtedly plays an essential role in sport.` |
| `finish their subject` | "比赛项目"不是 subject | `perform successfully`；比赛项目是 `event`（a swimming event） |
| `fight against other competitors` | 像真要打架 | `compete against other athletes` |
| `sports with high physicality` | 拼写 + 不自然 | `physically demanding sports`（直接记住） |

**Stephen Hawking 例子：建议整个删掉。** 三个问题：
- `mental power` 不如 `mental strength` / `determination`
- `fill the gap of physical power` 中式 → `compensate for a lack of physical ability`
- `golden prize in Olympics` → `an Olympic gold medal`

更好、更学术的替代论证：
> No matter how mentally determined an athlete is, it is difficult to succeed in a physically demanding sport without sufficient strength, speed or endurance.

> **IELTS 观念：例子不是越具体越好**，合理的 generic example 比真人轶事安全得多。

### Body 2（整篇最好，逻辑完全保留）

> Professional 身体素质差距小 → 心理成变量 → 压力下能否发挥潜力 → tennis example

| 原句 | 问题 | 改法 |
|------|------|------|
| `hardly trained` | **经典陷阱**：hardly=几乎不，意思是"几乎没训练" | `well trained` / `highly trained`（hard ≠ hardly） |
| `mental strength is more emphasised` | 同 Intro | `mental strength becomes more important` / `becomes a decisive factor` |
| `realtime state of competition` | 程序员英语 | `less affected by pressure during a competition` / `better able to remain calm under pressure` |
| `leading to a clear head` | 可更正式 | `allowing them to remain clear-headed` |
| `releasing the most of their physical potential` | 搭配不对 | `realise their full potential` / `perform to the best of their ability` |
| `In this circumstances` | 复数 | `In these circumstances`（与上句重复，可直接删） |

tennis example 只需修句型（`repeated mistakes` / `crucial moments` 用得不错）：
> For example, **an outstanding tennis player with poor mental resilience** may still lose a match if anxiety causes them to make repeated mistakes at crucial moments.

### Conclusion

| 原句 | 问题 | 改法 |
|------|------|------|
| `although both physical strength provides` | both 后面要有两个东西，这里只有 physical | `Although physical strength provides...` |
| `the fundamental` | fundamental 是形容词 | `the foundation`（名词） |
| `achieving higher performance` | 搭配 | `achieving greater success` / `consistent success` |
| `among the athletes` | 泛指不加 the | `among athletes` |

## 保留原思路的改稿

> People have different views about whether physical or mental strength plays a more important role in sporting success. While physical strength is clearly essential in many sports, I believe mental strength is more important, particularly at higher levels of competition.
>
> Physical ability undoubtedly plays an essential role in sport. Athletes need sufficient physical strength, speed and endurance to perform successfully and compete against others, especially in physically demanding sports. Mental determination can never completely compensate for a lack of physical ability. No matter how mentally strong an athlete is, it is difficult to succeed if they do not possess the physical capabilities required by their sport.
>
> On the other hand, mental strength often becomes the decisive factor in a competition. Professional athletes are usually highly trained, giving them relatively similar levels of physical ability. In this case, mental strength becomes increasingly important. Athletes with greater mental resilience may be better able to remain calm under pressure, allowing them to stay clear-headed and perform to the best of their physical ability. For example, an outstanding tennis player with poor mental resilience may still lose a match if anxiety causes them to make repeated mistakes at crucial moments. Therefore, mental strength can play a more important role than physical ability at the highest levels of competition.
>
> In conclusion, although physical strength provides the foundation for athletic performance, I believe that mental strength is more important for achieving consistent success, especially among athletes competing at higher levels.

论点、顺序、tennis example 都没动，只把英文修自然。

## 核心规律：collocation / lexical choice

你认识的词（cost, subject, fight, emphasise, hardly, release, fundamental）全都在，问题是：
> **拿到了"意思大概接近"的英文词，但不知道母语者在这个语境下到底用不用它。**

所以训练重点不是狂背高级词，而是：

- 不要记 `pressure = 压力`，直接记 **cope with pressure / perform under pressure / remain calm under pressure**
- 不要记 `potential = 潜力`，直接记 **realise one's full potential**
- 不要记 `competition = 比赛`，直接记 **compete against other athletes / at the highest level of competition**

写的时候大脑一次吐出一整块，而不是一个词一个词拼（这也解释了你"写得折磨"的原因）。

## 练法建议

> 每天完整写一篇，让 GPT 把"不自然但能理解"的表达全部抓出来，专门复习这些表达。连续十篇，会发现错误反复就是那二三十种。
