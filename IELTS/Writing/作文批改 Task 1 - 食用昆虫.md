# 作文批改 · Task 1 · 食用昆虫

> 题目：地图（各国对食用昆虫制品的接受度）+ 柱状图（昆虫食品 vs 常规食品价格）· 2026-08-30 批改
> 上一篇：[[作文批改 Task 1 - 世界人口占比]]
> 结构 / 改写 / 分组的通用方法见 [[Task 1 写法]]

## 总评

- 约 **269 词，偏长**；Task 1 控制在 **180–210 词**更稳
- 大结构已经是对的：Introduction → Overview → Body 1 地图 → Body 2 价格；**不是"没思路"**
- 真正缺的是**信息筛选和分组**：把地图里几乎所有国家的数据依次列了一遍
- 分项预估：TA 6.5 / CC 6.0–6.5 / LR 5.5–6.0 / GRA 5.5 → **综合约 6.0**
- 内容选择已及格，主要被语言错误密度压分

## 做得好的地方

- Overview 抓到两个核心趋势：多数国家不愿意 > 愿意；昆虫食品普遍更贵
- 正确找到 Mexico 最高、Japan 最低、weaver ant 最贵、female giant water bug 最便宜（昆虫类）、beef 最贵（常规类）
- Brazil / China 不到 50% 但地图标黄（愿意 > 不愿意）——读图正确：可能还有中立回答，**不能自行把 50% 当唯一分界线**

## 最主要的问题：不要列完所有国家

> In South Africa the number is 39%, followed by 36% in UK, 35% in USA, 34% in Australia, 33% in Spain and India, 32% in Russia and 21% in Japan...

信息对，但题目要求 **selecting and reporting the main features**，不是报每个数。分组一句搞定：

> In all the remaining countries, willingness was below 40%, ranging from 39% in South Africa to only 21% in Japan.

考前草稿只列骨架：

- 地图：Mexico 71% 最高 → Peru 58% / Thailand 56% 次高 → 其余 21%–39% → Japan 21% 最低；只有五个国家愿意者多于不愿意者
- 价格：常规食品 $0.78–$3.03/kg；昆虫食品 $2.60–$10.65/kg；最贵常规 beef，最便宜常规 Thai rice；最便宜昆虫 female giant water bug，最贵 weaver ant

## 分段批改

### Introduction

| 原句 | 问题 | 改法 |
|------|------|------|
| `The map and charts here shows` | 一张 map + 一张 chart；复数主语用 `show`；`here` 多余 | `The map shows ..., while the bar chart compares ...` |
| `the current price for certain food available on the market place` | certain food 不自然；marketplace 拼写 / on the market 更自然 | `the current market prices of selected conventional and insect-based foods` |

改写：

> The map shows the percentages of people willing to eat insect-based products in 13 countries, while the bar chart compares the current market prices of selected conventional and insect-based foods.

时态：没有历史年份、价格是 current，主体用**一般现在时**完全正确（本篇时态不是问题）。

### Overview

| 原句 | 问题 | 改法 |
|------|------|------|
| `the willingness ... is lower than unwillingness in most countries` | 太抽象 | `Only five countries record more willing than unwilling respondents.` |
| `Most countries with high willingness ... are in Latin America, East Asia and South East Asia` | 像在概括全世界，图里只有 13 国 | `The highest willingness rates are concentrated in Latin America and parts of Asia.` |
| `South East Asia` | 拼写 | `Southeast Asia` |

更完整的 Overview：

> Overall, willingness to consume insect-based products varies considerably among the countries shown, with Mexico recording the highest figure and Japan the lowest. Only five countries have more willing than unwilling respondents. In addition, insect-based products are generally considerably more expensive than conventional foods.

### 地图段

| 原句 | 问题 | 改法 |
|------|------|------|
| `The willingness to eat insect foods of Mexico is the highest which is 71%` | 国家本身没有"意愿" | `Mexico records the highest willingness rate, at 71%.`（好用的结构：**A records the highest figure, at X%.**） |
| `The Willingness of Peru, Thailand... are 58%, 56%...` | willingness 单数；该用 figures | `The corresponding figures for Peru, Thailand, Brazil and China are 58%, 56%, 45% and 44% respectively.` 更有分组感：`Peru and Thailand follow at 58% and 56% respectively, while the figures for Brazil and China are more moderate, at 45% and 44%.` |
| `The willingness of these countries is higher than unwillingness and lower in other countries` | 表达 | `These are the only five countries in which willing respondents outnumber unwilling ones; the reverse is true elsewhere.` |

### 价格段

| 原句 | 问题 | 改法 |
|------|------|------|
| `the prices of insect based foods are commonly higher than other common foods` | commonly 不合适；要加连字符 | `Insect-based foods are generally more expensive than conventional products.` |
| `Beef is the most expensive common foods now` | 一种食品用 food；`which costs` 啰嗦 | `Beef is the most expensive conventional food, at $3.03 per kilogram.` |
| `famale wild giant water bug` | 拼写 female；词序 | `the female giant water bug`（直接按图表标签最稳） |
| `which is even three times of beef` | 比较结构错误 | `more than three times the price of beef` / `over three times as expensive as beef`（10.65 ÷ 3.03 ≈ 3.5，要 **more than** three times） |

## 更有效的数据选择：报范围，不报排名

原文报了 beef 第一、buffalo 第二、water bug 最便宜、cricket 第二便宜、weaver ant 最贵——没错但低效。直接报**两组的价格范围**：

> Conventional foods range from $0.78 to $3.03 per kilogram, whereas insect-based products range from $2.60 to $10.65.

一句话给出两组的最低、最高和整体差距，再补一句 `The wild weaver ant is the most expensive product overall.` 即可。

## 参考修订版（约 190 词）

> The map shows the percentages of people willing to eat insect-based products in 13 countries, while the bar chart compares the current prices of selected conventional and insect-based foods.
>
> Overall, willingness varies considerably among the countries shown, with Mexico recording the highest figure and Japan the lowest. Only five countries record more willing than unwilling respondents. In addition, insect-based foods are generally more expensive than conventional products.
>
> Mexico has by far the highest willingness rate, at 71%, followed by Peru and Thailand at 58% and 56% respectively. Brazil and China record more moderate figures of 45% and 44%. In all the remaining countries, willingness is below 40%, ranging from 39% in South Africa to just 21% in Japan.
>
> The prices of conventional foods range from $0.78 per kilogram for Thai Hom Mali rice to $3.03 for beef. By contrast, insect-based products cost between $2.60 and $10.65 per kilogram. The female giant water bug is the cheapest insect product, whereas the wild weaver ant is the most expensive product overall. At $10.65 per kilogram, the latter costs more than three times the price of beef.

190 词比原来 269 词覆盖得还清楚。

## 该记的表达

不需要背昆虫名称，记这些可复用的 Task 1 表达（已收进 [[IELTS/Vocabulary/写作主动词汇|写作主动词汇]]）：

`the countries shown` · `record the highest/lowest figure` · `follow at X%` · `the corresponding figure` · `range from X to Y` · `at $X per kilogram` · `insect-based products` · `conventional foods` · `more than three times the price of...` · `willing respondents outnumber unwilling ones`

## 核心结论

> **Task 1 没有"想不出观点"的问题，只有"没有找好分组"的问题。**

两张不太相关的图：Overview 各总结一句，Body 1 写第一张，Body 2 写第二张，不必强行找两张图之间的因果联系。抄题干、"有头没尾"等通用问题见 [[Task 1 写法]]。
