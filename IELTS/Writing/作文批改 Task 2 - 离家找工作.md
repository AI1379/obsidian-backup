## 分段批改

### Introduction

| 原句 | 问题 | 改法 |
|------|------|------|
| `which is gaining more and more debate` | 不自然 | `which has attracted increasing debate`；`Nowadays` 不必要 |
| 整段 | 可以更简单 | `In many countries, people have to move away from their families and friends in order to find suitable employment. Although this trend has some disadvantages, I believe that its benefits outweigh the drawbacks.` |

### 第二段：disadvantages

| 原句 | 问题 | 改法 |
|------|------|------|
| `finding jobs in other city and be far from friends` | 平行结构 + other→another | `There are several disadvantages to finding work in another city and living far away from family and friends.` |
| `people may need supports` | support 不可数 | `need support`（同族：time and effort 不可数，直接记） |
| `at the first periods after graduation` | 不自然 | `during the first few months after graduation` / `in the early stages of their careers`（后者特别适合本题） |
| `adapt themselves to their jobs` | adapt to sth 即可 | `adapt to their new environment` / `adjust to a new job and lifestyle` |
| `Loneliness is another result that may caused by moving away` | may **be** caused；更自然是换个主语 | `Moving away from home may also lead to loneliness.` |
| `negative effects to their mental health` | 固定搭配 | `have a negative effect on their mental health` |

整段可以这样写：

> Living away from family and friends can create several difficulties. Young graduates may need emotional or practical support from their families, particularly during the early stages of their careers. They may also need more time and effort to adjust to a new job, a new city and an unfamiliar lifestyle. In addition, living far from close friends and relatives may lead to loneliness, which can have a negative effect on mental health.

### 第三段：全文最好的一段

论证链很好：大城市机会多 → 职业发展 → 重建社交圈 → 网络缓解离家损失。

| 原句 | 问题 | 改法 |
|------|------|------|
| `there are much more job opportunity` | opportunity 可数 | `many more job opportunities`（典型规则：much more + 不可数 / many more + 可数复数，如 much more money / many more jobs） |
| `more chance to find a proper job` | proper→suitable | `more opportunities to find a suitable job` |
| `achieve a more successful career` | 搭配 | `build a successful career` / `develop their careers` |
| `discard the barriers thay have made during the student periods` | 全文最不自然，可降维 | `Moving to a new city also allows young people to leave their familiar environment, experience a different lifestyle and form new social connections.` / `step outside their comfort zone and meet people from different backgrounds` |
| `create new social connections with another kind of people` | 直译 | `form new social connections with people from different backgrounds` |
| `with the development of modern Internet` | 要 the Internet；更自然 | `Thanks to modern communication technology` |
| `get in touch with family and friends` | get in touch = 联系上；"保持联系"是 | `stay in touch with`（区别值得记） |
| `weaken some negative effects` | 能懂但不自然 | `reduce the negative effects`（mitigate 稍高级，可不记） |

### Conclusion

把理由带回来，别只重复 introduction：

> In conclusion, although moving away from family and friends can cause practical and emotional difficulties, I believe that the greater employment opportunities and personal development it offers make the advantages more significant overall.

## 保留原思路的改稿（接近稳 6.5–7，词汇没有突然变难）

> In many countries, people have to move away from their families and friends in order to find suitable employment. Although this trend has some disadvantages, I believe that its benefits outweigh the drawbacks.
>
> Living away from family and friends can create several difficulties. Young graduates may need emotional or practical support from their families, particularly during the early stages of their careers. Those who move to another city may also need more time and effort to adjust to a new job and an unfamiliar lifestyle. In addition, living far from close friends and relatives may lead to loneliness, which can have a negative effect on mental health.
>
> However, moving away from home can provide considerably greater opportunities. Large cities such as New York or Shanghai usually offer many more job opportunities than smaller towns. Therefore, people who move to major cities may have a better chance of finding suitable employment and building a successful career. Moving to a new city can also encourage young people to step outside their comfort zone, experience a different lifestyle and form new social connections with people from different backgrounds. Furthermore, thanks to modern communication technology, they can still stay in touch with their families and friends, which can reduce some of the negative effects of living far away.
>
> In conclusion, although moving away from family and friends can create emotional and practical difficulties, I believe that the greater career opportunities and personal development it offers mean that the advantages outweigh the disadvantages.
