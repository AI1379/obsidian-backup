## 分句批改

| 原句 | 问题 | 改法 |
|------|------|------|
| `This diagram illustrated the process...` | 时态 | `The diagram illustrates...`（图在眼前，用现在时） |
| `about 9 steps` | 无谓地冒险数步骤 | `consisting of several stages`（最安全） |
| `ending with transporting` | 不自然 | `ending with the storage and transportation of the finished product` |
| `noticable` / `seperated` | 拼写 | `noticeable` / `separated` |
| `solid by-product is separated from liquid after separating` | 语义重复（"分离之后被分离出来"） | `a solid by-product is removed during the separation stage` |
| `corn are stored` | corn 不可数 | `corn is stored` |
| `then is then cooked` | 笔误 | `is then cooked`（最后两分钟能抓出来） |
| `approprate amount of water` | 拼写 + 冠词 | `an appropriate amount of water`；但图没说"appropriate"，不要自行加信息 → `The milled corn is then cooked for four hours, with water being added during this stage.` |
| `transfered` | 拼写 | `transferred`（双写 r） |
| `to fermentation step` | 不自然 | `to the fermentation stage` |
| `costs about 5 hours` | **中文"花费时间"诱导用 cost** | `takes five hours`（cost 偏花钱：`The project cost $5 million.`） |
| `transported to is destination` | 笔误 | `its destination`（its=它的 / it's=it is） |

## 保留原语言的改稿

> The diagram illustrates the process by which ethanol fuel is produced from corn.
>
> Overall, the production of ethanol fuel is a linear process consisting of several stages, beginning with the storage and milling of corn and ending with the storage and transportation of the finished product. It is noticeable that water is added during cooking, while a solid by-product is removed during the separation stage.
>
> Initially, harvested corn is stored before being milled. The milled corn is then cooked for about four hours, with water being added during this stage. Once the cooking stage has been completed, the mixture is transferred to the fermentation stage and left for 48 hours. After fermentation, the mixture is separated into a solid by-product and a liquid. The liquid then proceeds to the next stage, where it is purified for five hours.
>
> Following purification, the resulting ethanol is stored. Finally, the ethanol fuel is transported to its destination.
