## Introduction

- `under different circumstances` 太模糊。图有三个明确维度：developing/developed · 1990/1998 · 三级教育
- 图展示的是 **the number of girls per 100 boys**，不是简单的数量比较
- 准确版：
  > The chart compares the number of girls per 100 boys enrolled in primary, secondary and tertiary education in developing and developed countries in 1990 and 1998.

> **Task 1 Intro 核心原则：准确 > 华丽。**

## Overview

- `developed countries or 1998` 用 `or` 拼两个不同维度，读不懂。要拆成两个事实：
  > Overall, the figures were consistently higher in developed countries than in developing countries, and all three education levels recorded higher ratios in 1998 than in 1990.
- `increasement` → `increase`；更自然的是 `As the level of education increased, the number of girls per 100 boys rose in developed countries but fell in developing countries.`
- 数据是 1990/1998 → 描述数据用**过去时**（increased / rose / fell），不要 increases
- **漏掉的 Main Feature：Target = 100**（100 = 男女入学相等）。developing 全部低于 100，developed tertiary 超 100。完整版：
  > Overall, the figures were consistently higher in developed countries than in developing countries, and they increased between 1990 and 1998 in every category. Moreover, as the level of education rose, the ratio increased in developed countries but declined in developing countries. Only tertiary education in developed countries exceeded the target of 100 girls per 100 boys.

## Body

| 原句 | 问题 | 改法 |
|------|------|------|
| `In the stage of primary education` | 不自然 | `At the primary level`（Task 1 高频，直接记成 chunk） |
| `the numbers...is close to 100` | 主谓不一致 | 推荐用神词 `the figure`：`the figures for developing countries were 83 and 87 in 1990 and 1998 respectively` |
| `close to 100`（指 83/87） | 83 距离目标明显 | 用 `compared with` 一句比较四个数：`At the primary level, developing countries recorded 83 girls per 100 boys in 1990 and 87 in 1998, compared with 95 and 96 respectively in developed countries.` |
| `Coming to tertiary education` | 口语感强 | `At the tertiary level` |
| `the numbers...becomes even lower` | 时态/用词 | `The contrast was even greater at the tertiary level, with figures of 66 and 75 in developing countries, compared with 105 and 112 in developed countries.` |
| `girls are more than boys` | 中文直译 | `there were more girls than boys` / `girls outnumbered boys`（`outnumber` 值得记） |
| `educaion` | 拼写 | `education` |

Secondary 写得不错，只改时态 + figures：
> At the secondary level, the figures fell to 72 and 82 in developing countries, whereas they rose to 98 and 99 in developed countries.

（`whereas` 特别适合"一边降一边升"，比不停用 but 自然。）

## 保留原语言的改稿

> The chart compares the number of girls per 100 boys enrolled in primary, secondary and tertiary education in developing and developed countries in 1990 and 1998.
>
> Overall, the figures were consistently higher in developed countries than in developing countries, and they increased between 1990 and 1998 in every category. Moreover, as the level of education rose, the number of girls per 100 boys increased in developed countries but decreased in developing countries.
>
> At the primary level, developing countries recorded 83 girls per 100 boys in 1990 and 87 in 1998, compared with 95 and 96 respectively in developed countries. At the secondary level, the figures fell to 72 and 82 in developing countries, whereas they rose to 98 and 99 in developed countries.
>
> The contrast was even greater at the tertiary level. Developing countries recorded only 66 girls per 100 boys in 1990 and 75 in 1998, while the corresponding figures for developed countries were 105 and 112. These were the only figures above the target of 100, meaning that girls outnumbered boys in tertiary education in developed countries in both years.

（约为 Band 7 左右写法，和原稿距离并不远。）

## 结论

- 信号比数字更重要：12 分钟 173 词，**12 个核心数据全对**，Overview 抓到真趋势 → Task 1 不是"不会写"的问题
- 要练到自动化的固定表达：
  > the figure for... / compared with... / respectively / whereas / while / at the primary/secondary/tertiary level / rose & fell from...to... / exceeded & remained below the target / outnumbered
- **速度建议**：12 分钟太快。正式考试允许自己 **15–17 分钟写 + 2–3 分钟检查**，`numbers...is`、`becomes`、`educaion` 这类错误最后两分钟扫一遍基本能抓出来
