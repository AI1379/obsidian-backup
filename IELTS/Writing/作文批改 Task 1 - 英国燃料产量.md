## 最先要修的：数据准确性

| 原文 | 图上实际 | 改法 |
|------|---------|------|
| petroleum 1981 = **100** | 约 **90** | `In 1981, petroleum production stood at approximately 90 energy units, compared with around 80 for coal and 38 for natural gas.` |
| coal 的 valley 在 **1983** | 更接近 **1984**（约 35） | `coal production fell sharply to a low of around 35 units` |
| petroleum **hit the bottom** in 1990 | 降到约 100，但**不是历史最低**（1981 约 90 更低） | `petroleum fell to a low of around 100 units in 1990`，不用 hit the bottom |

## 分段批改

### Introduction

| 原句 | 问题 | 改法 |
|------|------|------|
| `the main kinds of fuel containing petroleum, coal and natural gas` | containing 变成"包含……的燃料" | `The graph illustrates the production levels of three main fuels—petroleum, coal and natural gas—in the UK between 1981 and 2000.` |

### Overview：趋势抓得对，两处要调

- `petroleum was the main fuel produced` → `Petroleum remained the most highly produced fuel throughout the period.` / `Petroleum production remained considerably higher than that of the other two fuels.`
- coal 降 / gas 涨的观察正确 → `Overall, coal production declined considerably, whereas natural gas production rose, particularly after 1990.`（`whereas` 非常适合三线图）
- **单位说明句从 Overview 删掉**：`The production levels of fuel is measured by energy units...` 不是 main feature，Task 1 不要求解释纵轴单位定义，正文直接写 `140 energy units` 即可

理想 Overview：

> Overall, petroleum remained the dominant fuel throughout the period, despite considerable fluctuations. Coal production declined substantially overall, whereas natural gas remained relatively stable until around 1990 before rising sharply.

### 主体段最大的问题：三个 fuel 来回跳

原文 petroleum → coal → gas → petroleum → coal → ... 读者要一直切换对象。线图按**趋势相似程度**分组（见 [[Task 1 写法]]）：

- **Body 1 只写 petroleum**：90 → 140 → 100 → 140
- **Body 2 写 coal + natural gas**：coal 80 → 35 → 60 → 30；gas 约 40 稳定 → 1990 后涨到 100；gas 在 1990s 超过 coal（**交叉点值得写**）

### 表达

| 原句 | 问题 | 改法 |
|------|------|------|
| `kept stable` | 不自然 | `remained stable` / `remained relatively unchanged` |
| `there was a valley of the production level of coal` | 图表不用 valley | `coal production fell sharply to a low of around 35 units`（记 **fall to a low of** / **reach a peak of**） |
| `then decreases slowly` | 历史数据要过去时 | `then decreased gradually until 2000` |
| `petroleum decreases and hit the buttom` | 时态 + buttom 拼写；Task 1 推荐 a low 不用 bottom | `declined and reached a low of approximately 100 units` |
| `increased stably` | 搭配错误 | `increased steadily` |

**stable ≠ steadily**（重要）：

- stable = 水平基本不变 → `The figure remained stable at 40.`
- steadily = 变化稳定、持续 → `The figure increased steadily from 40 to 100.`

## 参考修订版（约 190 词）

> The graph illustrates the production levels of three main fuels—petroleum, coal and natural gas—in the UK between 1981 and 2000.
>
> Overall, petroleum remained the most highly produced fuel throughout the period, despite considerable fluctuations. By contrast, coal production declined substantially overall, while natural gas remained relatively stable until around 1990 before rising steadily.
>
> Petroleum production stood at approximately 90 energy units in 1981 and then rose sharply, reaching around 140 units in 1984. It remained close to this level for several years before falling to approximately 100 units in 1990. Thereafter, production recovered steadily, returning to around 140 units by 1996 and remaining broadly stable until 2000.
>
> Coal started at roughly 80 energy units and fell dramatically to about 35 in 1984. Although it recovered to around 60 units by 1986, its production subsequently declined gradually, ending the period at just over 30 units. Natural gas, meanwhile, remained at approximately 40 units throughout most of the 1980s. From around 1990 onwards, however, it rose steadily, overtaking coal in the early 1990s and eventually reaching 100 units in 2000.

词汇并不比原文难多少。

## 该记的线图词（已收进 [[IELTS/Vocabulary/写作主动词汇|写作主动词汇]]）

`rise / increase` · `fall / decline` · `remain stable` · `rise steadily` · `rise sharply` · `fall dramatically` · `reach a peak of` · `fall to a low of` · `recover to` · `fluctuate` · `stand at approximately X`

尤其记牢 **stable ≠ steadily**。
