> The bar charts illustrates the precentage of the total world population in India, China, USA and Japan in 1950 and 2002 and projections for 2050.

## 本篇核心：过去 + 未来预测的混合时态

图中 1950 / 2002 是过去，2050 是预测：

> China **accounted for** 23% in 1950 and **fell** to 20% in 2002. It **is projected to fall** further to 15% by 2050.

- `has decreased since 1950` 不适用：图只给了 1950 和 2002 两个节点，没有"持续到现在"的数据 → 写 `fell between 1950 and 2002`
- 2050 用 `is projected / predicted / expected to...`，比 `will` 严谨

时间轴直接记：

> **1950/2002 → past simple**
> **2050 → is projected / predicted / expected to**

## 分句批改

### Introduction

| 原句 | 问题 | 改法 |
|------|------|------|
| `The bar charts illustrates` | 只有一张图 + 主谓不一致 | `The bar chart illustrates` |
| `precentage` | 高频拼写错误，必须纠正 | `percentage` |
| `the total world population` | 稍生硬 | `the world's population` |

> The bar chart illustrates the percentage of the world's population living in India, China, the USA and Japan in 1950 and 2002, together with projections for 2050.

### Overview（三个核心信息都对）

| 原句 | 问题 | 改法 |
|------|------|------|
| `the number of USA almost keep the same` | 图展示的是 percentage/share 不是 number | `the figure for the USA is projected to remain stable` |
| `percentage was the most` | 搭配 | `China had the highest percentage` / `China accounted for the largest share` |
| `surpass` | 没问题，`overtake` 更有 Task 1 味 | `India is projected to overtake China by 2050`（surpass 不用换） |

压成的 Overview：
> Overall, the shares of the world population accounted for by China, the USA and Japan fell between 1950 and 2002, whereas India's figure increased. These trends are generally projected to continue to 2050, when India is expected to overtake China and become the largest of the four countries in terms of its share of the global population.

### Body

| 原句 | 问题 | 改法 |
|------|------|------|
| `which decreased to 20%` | which 易指向 the total world population | `before the figure fell to 20% in 2002` |
| `will decrease to about 15% in prediction for 2050` | `in prediction for 2050` 不自然 | `is predicted to fall to 15% by 2050` / `is projected to fall to 15% by 2050` |
| `Japan has the same trend` | | `Japan shows a similar pattern`（描述图中 pattern 可用现在时，数字变化用过去+预测混合：`with its share falling from 4% in 1950 to 3% in 2002 and projected to decline to around 1% in 2050`） |
| `while is predicted to keep the same in 2050` | 缺主语 + 搭配 | `while it is predicted to remain unchanged at around 5% in 2050` |
| `the only different one` | 不自然 | `India was the only country to show the opposite trend.` |
| `with about 15% population` | 不是"15% population" | `15% of the world's population` / `a share of 15%` |
| 逗号硬接两个完整句（comma splice） | 语法 | 拆成两句：`Its share rose from approximately 15% in 1950 to 17% in 2002 and is projected to reach around 19% in 2050.` |
| `become the country with the most population among these four` | 不精确 | `have the largest share of the world's population among the four countries` / `become the largest of the four in population terms` |

`remain` 值得练成主动词的同义组：**remain stable / remain unchanged / stay at approximately 5%**

## 保留原文的"考场级修正"版

> The bar chart illustrates the percentage of the world's population living in India, China, the USA and Japan in 1950 and 2002, together with projections for 2050.
>
> Overall, the percentages for China, the USA and Japan fell between 1950 and 2002, and those of China and Japan are projected to decrease further by 2050, while the figure for the USA is expected to remain stable. By contrast, India's share increased and is predicted to continue rising. China had the highest figure in 1950 and 2002, but India is projected to overtake it by 2050.
>
> In detail, China accounted for about 23% of the world's population in 1950, before the figure fell to 20% in 2002. It is projected to decline further to approximately 15% by 2050. Japan shows a similar pattern, with its share falling from about 4% in 1950 to 3% in 2002 and expected to reach only around 1% in 2050.
>
> The USA accounted for around 7% of the world's population in 1950 and 5% in 2002, with the latter figure predicted to remain unchanged in 2050. India was the only country to show the opposite trend, rising from approximately 15% in 1950 to 17% in 2002. Its share is projected to reach around 19% in 2050, making it the largest of the four countries.

## passive → active：本篇主动查过的两个词

`surpass` 和 `be predicted/projected to` 都符合 passive → active 转换点。下一篇出现类似场景时**强制自己不用查**，尝试把这两个调出来。第二次能自己想起，这次查词就值了。
